## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧠 FocusLens — AI Eye-Tracking Focus Monitor

An AI-powered attention monitoring application designed to help users understand their focus patterns, detect distractions, and build better study habits.

**Key Features**
- Eye and blink tracking for attention monitoring.
- Head-orientation and focus-score analysis.
- Attention leak detection and focus alerts.
- Burnout-pattern and posture monitoring.
- XP, streaks, and progress tracking.
- Authenticated dashboard for personal insights.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js, TypeScript, face-api.js, Clerk

---

### 🌾 Annapoorna AI — Intelligent Farming Assistant

An AI-powered agricultural platform designed to help farmers make informed decisions using farm-specific information and conversational assistance.

**Key Features**
- AI-powered agricultural chatbot and crop assistance.
- Image-based crop disease and symptom analysis.
- Weather information and mandi price insights.
- Government scheme discovery and farm planning.
- Farm diary, crop tracking, and activity logs.
- Multilingual assistance and personalized recommendations.

**Tech Stack:** React, JavaScript, Python, Groq, PostgreSQL, Neon, Cloudinary, JWT

---

### 📊 DataLens — Ask Questions. Understand Your Data.

A privacy-focused conversational data-analysis platform that helps users explore CSV datasets, generate insights, and understand their data using natural language.

**Key Features**
- Upload and inspect CSV datasets.
- Ask questions about data using natural language.
- Generate and validate analysis logic with an LLM.
- Produce data summaries, visualizations, and reports.
- Inspect schemas, data quality, and missing values.
- Explore results through an interactive dashboard.

**Tech Stack:** React, Vite, JavaScript, Python, FastAPI, Pandas, Groq

**Focus:** Privacy-aware data processing, validated analysis, and explainable results.
